## Business context (1 paragraph)
ARIE Finance Ltd is a Mauritius-regulated payment intermediary serving
internationally active businesses (cross-border payments, FX, treasury,
correspondent banking). Management needs the external developments that
genuinely matter to ARIE delivered to them daily, without searching. The
deliverable is a **management briefing** ("ARIE Digest"), consumable in
≤5 minutes — not an application.

## The product
Daily (weekdays, 08:15 Mauritius time / UTC+4; Monday includes weekend).
Visible sections, in order:
1. **What Matters Today** — max 3 bullets (most-regret-not-knowing items).
2. **Mauritius** — FSC, Bank of Mauritius, MoF, EDB, local regulation/commerce.
3. **Worldwide** — payments, cross-border finance, banking, FX, treasury,
   payment infrastructure, correspondent banking, de-risking, sanctions,
   AML/CFT, capital controls, payment networks, corridors, competitors, intl regulation.
4. **Film & TV** — productions, production finance, incentives/rebates,
   film commissions, cross-border production payments, shooting jurisdictions.
5. **Oil & Gas** — physical energy trading, refineries, LNG, trade finance,
   sanctions, shipping, commodity-payment restrictions, major projects,
   Africa/Middle East corridors.

## Item format (concise)
Headline · **Summary:** 1–2 factual sentences · **Why it matters to ARIE:** one
specific sentence · Source · date · link. Optional: `COMMERCIAL SIGNAL — [reason]`.
No scores, badges, owners, trends, actions, AI outreach, prospect recs.

## Commercial Signal guardrail (annotation only)
Add ONLY when ALL three exist: (1) named company/entity; (2) specific observable
trigger (intl expansion / new market / new foreign operation / intl acquisition /
major intl contract / relevant licence / fundraising tied to intl activity /
documented banking-payment difficulty); (3) credible, evidence-supported ARIE
cross-border/payments angle. Never a score, contact, or outreach recommendation.

## Volume
Normal 5–10; quiet 2–4; very quiet 0 (acceptable); busy ~12 max. Never fill a quota.

## Must-Catch (map into visible sections, not new tabs)
sanctions; AML/CFT; correspondent banking; de-risking; capital controls; FX
convertibility; payment-network changes; SWIFT/SEPA/rail developments; material
banking/payment outages; relevant cyber; bank/payment-company failure; material
licensing/enforcement; corridor restrictions/opportunities; trade-finance
restrictions; film incentive/rebate changes; production-finance; oil sanctions/
payment restrictions; direct ARIE/ACBM mentions; important competitor moves.

## Date & evidence discipline (critical)
Underlying event date governs freshness. Search/feed/index/syndication dates do
NOT prove event date. Prefer primary issuer/authority evidence; else strong
corroboration. Old news republished today is not new. Same event across
publishers = one story. Target: zero unsupported claims, zero stale-as-new.

## Noise to reject
routine Treasury instruments; procurement/tenders; job adverts; routine
statistics; generic fintech fundraising; generic crypto speculation;
casting/celebrity/box-office; oil-price chatter; irrelevant exploration;
unrelated earnings; resurfaced old news; syndicated duplicates; SEO guides;
generic opinion.

## Core AI decision
"Would a senior ARIE manager reasonably benefit from knowing this today because
it affects regulation, payments, banking, cross-border commerce, customers,
counterparties, corridors, competitors, Film/TV payment activity or Oil & Gas
trade/payment risk?" If weak → REJECT. Empty sections beat weak content.
